04About

Same words,
different people.

Street Script is a collection of real messages found across New York City: chalk on sidewalks, notes taped to lamp posts, words painted on utility boxes. Most of them wash away in the next rain.

Why it exists

Street writing has long been a way for people to share hope, thoughts and personal reflections in public spaces. Someone stops, writes a few words for a stranger, and walks away. Thousands of people walk past. A few of them really need it.

Street Script collects these small messages before they disappear, and asks the people who found them one question: what did it mean to you? The same three words can mean a job offer to one person and a breakup to another.

A project by Parissa Bansal, Parsons School of Design.
